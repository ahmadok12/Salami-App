SalamiBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Your Data Stays in Your Google Drive

SalamiBook is a client-side, open-source Progressive Web App (PWA). It has no backend servers and no central database. All wedding guest names, salami amounts, and configuration settings are stored locally on your device (via browser IndexedDB) and synced directly from your browser to a Google Sheet inside your own Google Drive account.

2. Minimal Google Permissions (drive.file)

When you sign in with Google, SalamiBook requests only the non-sensitive https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope (plus basic profile info for collector attribution). This strictly limits access to spreadsheets created by SalamiBook or explicitly selected by you via Google Picker. SalamiBook cannot view, modify, or access any other files in your Google Drive.

3. No Tracking, Analytics, or Data Sharing

We do not collect, sell, share, or transmit your personal or family data to any third party. Your use of Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Deleting Your Data

You can delete your data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for SalamiBook and deleting the wedding spreadsheet from your Google Drive. You can also revoke SalamiBook's access at any time from your Google Account Permissions page.
